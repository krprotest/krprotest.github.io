분석 단위

보수·우파 행위자(단체, 정당, 소모임, 개인 등)가 공적 공간 또는 미디어를 통해 수행하는 개별 집합행동, 담화적 저항, 내부 동원·학습 사건. 1행 = 1사건. 여기서 '보수·우파'는 온건 보수에서 극우까지를 아우르는 넓은 뜻으로 쓰며, 항의행동도 거리 시위만이 아니라 성명·기자회견 같은 담화적 항의와 세미나·강연 같은 내부 동원 행사를 포함하는 넓은 뜻으로 씁니다. 행위자의 성향 판정은 사전에 정하지 않고 자료가 쌓인 뒤 행위자 유형·의제를 기준으로 분석합니다.

수집 경로

보수 성향 인터넷 매체 20여 곳의 기사를 자동으로 수집해 행동어·의제어 기준으로 후보를 고른 뒤, 연구자가 기사 본문을 읽고 사건 여부를 판정하고 코딩합니다. 주류 언론 보도는 확인·보완 자료로 씁니다. 같은 사건을 여러 매체가 보도하면 1행으로 합치고 출처를 모두 기록합니다.

Korean Right-Wing Protest Tracker

This is a non-profit research project that records the protest events of conservative and right-wing actors in South Korea as an event-level database, in the tradition of protest event analysis. Protest is defined broadly to include discursive forms (statements, press conferences, petitions) and internal mobilization events (seminars, forums) alongside demonstrations. One row is one event. The data are updated automatically every day, and only events that have passed review are published.

What is recorded

Individual collective actions, discursive protests, and internal mobilization events carried out by conservative and right-wing actors (organizations, parties, small groups, individuals) in public space or through the media, as confirmed by news reports. Fourteen action codes are grouped into five categories: discursive (statements, press conferences, petitions, complaints, picketing), demonstrative (rallies and marches, one-person protests, protest visits, hunger strikes, online collective action), confrontational (sit-ins and occupations, self-immolation), violent (physical clashes), and internal mobilization (seminars, forums, lectures).

How the data are collected

Articles from about twenty conservative online outlets are collected automatically and screened with action and issue keywords. A researcher then reads each candidate article, decides whether it describes an event, and codes it by hand according to a published codebook. Mainstream media reports are used to verify and supplement. Multiple reports of the same event are merged into one row with all sources listed.

Limitations

Actions that were not reported in the news are not captured. Reporting practices of conservative outlets may over-represent the Seoul area and certain issues. Crowd sizes vary in reliability depending on the source, which is recorded alongside each figure.

Use, citation and corrections

If you wish to use or cite the data for research or reporting, or to request a correction, please contact us by e-mail.
