분석 단위

보수·우파 행위자(단체, 정당, 소모임, 개인 등)가 공적 공간 또는 미디어를 통해 수행하는 개별 항의행동, 담화적 저항, 내부 동원·학습 사건을 분석단위로 삼습니다. 기사가 아니라 사건을 세므로, 같은 사건을 여러 매체가 보도한 경우 하나의 사건으로 기록하고 출처를 모두 밝힙니다. 여기서 '보수·우파'는 온건 보수에서 극우까지를 아우르는 넓은 뜻으로 쓰며, 항의행동도 거리 시위만이 아니라 성명·기자회견 같은 담화적 항의와 세미나·강연 같은 내부 동원 행사를 포함하는 넓은 뜻으로 씁니다. 행위자의 성향 판정은 사전에 정하지 않고 자료가 쌓인 뒤 행위자 유형·의제를 기준으로 분석합니다.

규모

경찰 추산을 우선하고, 없으면 언론 보도, 주최 측 주장, 사진 기반 추정 순으로 기록하며 출처를 함께 표기합니다. 성명·서명·고소처럼 참가 인원 개념이 없는 행동은 '비해당'입니다. 인원이 보도되지 않았고 사진 등으로도 추정할 수 없으면 '미상'으로 둡니다.

한계

언론에 보도되지 않은 행동은 포착되지 않습니다. 보수 매체의 보도 관행에 따라 수도권과 특정 의제가 과대 대표될 수 있습니다. 인원 수치는 출처에 따라 신뢰도가 다르므로 size_source를 함께 보아야 합니다.

Methodology

Unit of analysis

An individual protest action, discursive protest, or internal mobilization event carried out by a conservative or right-wing actor (organization, party, small group or individual) in public space or through the media is the unit of analysis. Events rather than articles are counted: when several outlets report the same event, it is recorded as a single event and all sources are listed. 'Conservative and right-wing' is used broadly, from moderate conservatives to the far right. Protest is also used broadly: besides street demonstrations it includes discursive forms such as statements and press conferences, and internal mobilization events such as seminars and lectures. Actors are not classified by ideology in advance; analysis by actor type and issue follows once the data accumulate.

Data collection

Articles from about twenty conservative online outlets are collected automatically and screened with action and issue keywords. A researcher then reads each candidate article, decides whether it describes an event, and codes it by hand. Mainstream media reports are used to verify and supplement.

Crowd size

Police estimates are preferred; otherwise news reports, organizers' claims and photo-based estimates by the coder are used, in that order, with the source recorded. Actions without participants in the usual sense, such as statements, petitions and legal complaints, are coded as not applicable. When no figure is reported and none can be estimated from photos, size is coded as unknown.

Limitations

Actions that were not reported in the news are not captured. Reporting practices of conservative outlets may over-represent the Seoul area and certain issues. Crowd sizes vary in reliability depending on the source, which is shown alongside each figure.

About

This is a non-profit research project that records the protest events of conservative and right-wing actors in South Korea (from moderate conservatives to the far right) as an event-level database, in the tradition of protest event analysis. The data are updated automatically every day, and events are published once they have been reviewed.

Language

In the English view, field names, codes and statistics are in English, while event titles, organizations, places, targets and outlet names remain in Korean as reported.

Corrections and removal requests

Factual errors are corrected once confirmed. Records based on published news reports are kept, but requests concerning personal information will be reviewed. Please send requests to the e-mail address below.

Following new events

Add feed.xml to an RSS reader to receive newly published events. After setting filters on the events page, the address in the browser bar can be shared to show the same selection; add ?lang=en to open the page in English.
